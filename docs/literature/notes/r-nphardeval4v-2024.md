# NPHardEval4V: A Dynamic Reasoning Benchmark of Multimodal Large Language Models

- **Citation:** Li X, Hua W, Zhu K, et al., arXiv 2024. arXiv:2403.01777.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.01777 · **Open access:** abstract only
- **Code:** https://github.com/lizhouf/NPHardEval4 · **Code licence:** unknown
- **Paper licence:** CC BY 4.0

## Mechanism
Benchmark of vision-language models on visual versions of four NP-hard problems (Knapsack, Set Cover, TSP, Vertex Cover).

## Evidence
No headline numbers seen; models do fine on perception but struggle with global optimisation and constraints.
