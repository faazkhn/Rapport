# CARS (PatientHub simulator): CBT client simulator, paper not confirmed

- **Citation:** No direct citation in PatientHub. Likely Qin et al. 2026, "When clients stop following: a cognitive conceptualization diagram-driven framework for strategic counseling", arXiv:2606.04389 (inferred, unverified, not read).
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11684 (PatientHub, which describes CARS) · **Open access:** abstract only (PatientHub HTML mentions)
- **Code:** https://github.com/Sahandfer/PatientHub (implementation) · **Code licence:** MIT
- **Paper licence:** unknown
- **Informs:** identity/formulation, behaviour/resistance

## Mechanism
Single-agent prompting CBT client. A generator expands a cognitive conceptualization diagram into a full profile. It is the only PatientHub method that can end a session itself.

## Evidence
Averages 5.1 of 15 turns before ending in PatientHub's comparison. Nothing else verified.

## Limitations
- Original paper not found or confirmed.
- Counselling, not psychiatry.

## For Rapport
**Verdict:** idea-only. Client able to end the session when disengaged is a useful behaviour/resistance idea (Twin could emit a walk-out behaviour at very low trust); paper unconfirmed.
