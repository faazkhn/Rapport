# Holderried 2024: A Language Model-Powered Simulated Patient With Automated Feedback for History Taking

- **Citation:** Tübingen group, JMIR Medical Education 10:e59213, 2024. DOI 10.2196/59213, PMID 39150749. (Author list not retrieved.)
- **Link:** https://mededu.jmir.org/2024/1/e59213 · **Open access:** abstract only (page text not retrievable; read via search listing)

- **Informs:** feedback/pedagogy, evaluation

## Mechanism
GPT-based chatbot simulates patient answers to medical students and gives immediate feedback on history-taking comprehensiveness. Feedback compared against a human rater.

## Evidence
106 conversations, 1894 question-answer pairs, mostly third-year students. Agreement with human raters reported as good overall; specific kappa values not read.

## Limitations
- Feedback less specific for some categories.
- Numbers not verified from full text.

## For Rapport
**Verdict:** idea-only. Post-session checklist-based LLM feedback validated against a human rater is a template for the feedback/evaluation layer, run offline after the encounter.
