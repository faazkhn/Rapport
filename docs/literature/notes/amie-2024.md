# AMIE: Towards Conversational Diagnostic AI

- **Citation:** Tu T, Palepu A, Schaekermann M, et al., arXiv 2401.05654, 2024.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.05654 · **Open access:** yes (full text read)
- **Code:** none (paper's Code Availability section: not open-sourcing model code) · **Code licence:** none
- **Paper licence:** arXiv non-exclusive
- **Informs:** learning, evaluation, feedback/pedagogy

## Mechanism
PaLM-2 based doctor model fine-tuned on real dialogue and medical QA, then on self-play simulated dialogues. Inner loop: four roles all played by AMIE: patient (vignette in the prompt, "respond honestly, ask questions"), doctor (empathetic, at most two questions per turn), moderator (yes/no: has the conversation ended), and a critic that knows the true diagnosis and gives in-context feedback on empathy, brevity, no repeats, at least two differentials; the doctor re-runs the conversation twice with that feedback. Vignettes: for a condition, retrieve 60 web passages, LLM-filter them, generate 2 vignettes with a one-shot format. Outer loop: refined dialogues (11,686 per iteration, 5,230 conditions, about 21 turns each) feed the next fine-tune. At inference the doctor uses a three-step chain per turn: analyse (summarise findings, differential, missing info, confidence), formulate reply, then refine for factuality, empathy and format (3 sequential calls).

## Evidence
- Randomised double-blind crossover remote OSCE: 20 PCPs vs AMIE, 20 patient actors, 149 scenarios (Canada 60, India 75, UK 14; psychiatry and paediatrics excluded), text chat.
- AMIE higher top-k differential accuracy than PCPs for all k (p<0.05 after FDR, bootstrap n=10,000); superior on 28 of 32 specialist-rated axes and 24 of 26 patient-actor axes.
- Information gathering was equivalent: accuracy plateaued within about 10 turns for both; AMIE's advantage came from interpreting information.
- Auto-evaluator (self-CoT, PACES axes) agreed with specialist ratings; dialogues after self-play critique were preferred over before.

## Limitations
- Unfamiliar synchronous text chat is unrepresentative of clinical practice; PCPs were handicapped.
- Scenario packs by patient actors, no real patients; no psychiatry; critic needs the ground-truth diagnosis.
- Model, code and prompts for the full system are not released; fine-tuning scale is out of reach for a local app.
- Rating axes are a pilot framework; no external validation of the critique loop on patient realism.

## For Rapport
**Verdict:** idea-only. Not about simulating the patient, but its self-play recipe (vignette generator, critic with ground truth giving in-context feedback, re-run, keep the best) is the template for Rapport's self-play doctors and the nightly experience-library curation, with the critic's rubric (empathy, no repeated questions) reused as an offline gate. The 3-call reasoning chain breaks the one-call live budget, so confine it to offline generation.
