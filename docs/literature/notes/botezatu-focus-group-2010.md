# Botezatu 2010: Virtual patient simulation: what do students make of it? A focus group study

- **Citation:** Botezatu M, Hult H, Fors UG. BMC Medical Education, 2010. DOI 10.1186/1472-6920-10-91
- **Link:** https://bmcmededuc.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/1472-6920-10-91 · **Open access:** yes (abstract read via Europe PMC; full text not read)
- **Code:** none found · **Code licence:** none
- **Paper licence:** CC BY
- **Informs:** feedback/pedagogy

## Mechanism
Qualitative content analysis of two focus groups of medical students who had used Web-SP (a non-LLM web virtual patient system) in an internal medicine course in Bogota. Interviews in Spanish, translated, double-coded.

## Evidence
2 focus groups, 8 students each; 18 categories in 5 themes: learning, teaching, assessment, authenticity, implementation. Authenticity was tied to case realism, local socio-cultural context, and presence and quality of feedback.

## Limitations
- One institution, 16 students, selected users.
- Pre-LLM scripted virtual patient; not about conversational realism.
- Self-report only.

## For Rapport
**Verdict:** idea-only. Supports that realism plus quality feedback drive perceived authenticity; informs the feedback/Twin-view design, no code.
