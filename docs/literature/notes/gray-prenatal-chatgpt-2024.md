# Increasing Realism and Variety of Virtual Patient Dialogues for Prenatal Counseling Education Through a Novel Application of ChatGPT

- **Citation:** Gray M., et al., JMIR Medical Education 10:e50705, 2024. DOI 10.2196/50705
- **Link:** https://mededu.jmir.org/2024/1/e50705/ · **Open access:** yes (PMC10870212 results read)

## Mechanism
ChatGPT 3.5 listed parental concerns about possible preterm delivery at 24 weeks, then wrote parent-physician role-plays for an anxious and a distrustful parent. Two neonatologists rated sentences on a 5-point scale.

## Evidence
35 role-plays, 176 unique parent responses, 254 sentences rated. Realism 5/5 for 72% (4-5 for 80%); relevance 5/5 for 85%; usability 5/5 for 50%, unusable 2%. Weighted kappa 0.84 on first 10%. 65% of responses (117/176) expressed an emotion.

## Limitations
- One model (ChatGPT 3.5, Feb 2023), two expert raters, no trainees or families.
- Short dialogues (about 5 exchanges).
- Possible factual errors; abstract and table counts differ (268 vs 254).

## For Rapport
**Verdict:** idea-only. Persona-conditioned (anxious vs distrustful) generation with expert sentence rating is a cheap template for faculty rating of Digital Twin replies.
