# VP survey 2007: Virtual Patient Simulation at U.S. and Canadian Medical Schools

- **Citation:** Huang G, Reynolds R, Candler C, Academic Medicine 82(5):446-451, 2007. PMID 17457063.
- **Link:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17457063/ · **Open access:** abstract only (PubMed abstract plus secondary news coverage; ResearchGate page would not load)

## Mechanism
Survey of 142 US and Canadian medical schools (Feb-Sep 2005) on how they build and share virtual patients (VPs). Descriptive, no system built.

## Evidence
108 schools responded; 26 produced VPs. Per press coverage, a third of institutions reported cost per VP above $50,000 and 87% above $10,000. Cases skewed to primary care and lacked ethnic diversity.

## Limitations
- Only institution-built VPs; industry VPs excluded.
- 2005 data, pre-LLM; scripted media-rich cases.
