# Campillos-Llanos 2021: Lessons Learned from the Usability Evaluation of a Simulated Patient Dialogue System

- **Citation:** Campillos-Llanos L, Thomas C, Bilinski E, et al. J Med Syst 45(7):69, 2021. DOI 10.1007/s10916-021-01737-4
- **Link:** https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10916-021-01737-4 · **Open access:** abstract only (Springer page blocked; abstract via HAL/search)

- **Informs:** evaluation, feedback/pedagogy

## Mechanism
French rule/frame-based virtual patient dialogue system where trainers add new cases that are processed without human intervention. Evaluated on usability with medical evaluators.

## Evidence
18 medical evaluators, 29 unseen cases vs 6 development cases. Mean user score 4.06/5 on unseen cases. About 74% of replies correct, ~15% incorrect, ~11% deferred by the system.

## Limitations
- Abstract only; details unverified.
- Rule/frame-based, not generative; French only.
- Evaluators were clinicians, not learners.

## For Rapport
**Verdict:** idea-only. The "deferred reply" category (system declines rather than answers wrongly) and per-reply correct/incorrect/deferred rating are a cheap eval scheme for the Digital Twin's locked-memory and out-of-case answers.
