# Yamamoto 2024: Enhancing Medical Interview Skills Through AI-Simulated Patient Interactions (nonrandomized controlled trial)

- **Citation:** Yamamoto A et al. JMIR Medical Education 10:e58753, 2024. DOI 10.2196/58753
- **Link:** https://mededu.jmir.org/2024/1/e58753 · **Open access:** yes, CC BY (abstract-level details only from search summary; full text fetch failed)
- **Code:** none found · **Code licence:** none
- **Paper licence:** CC BY 4.0
- **Informs:** evaluation, feedback/pedagogy

## Mechanism
LLM-based simulated-patient platform given to 4th-year Japanese medical students for interview practice; effect measured on the national pre-clinical-clerkship OSCE medical-interview score versus a prior-year cohort.

## Evidence
Intervention n=35 (2023) vs control n=110 (2022). OSCE interview score 28.1 (SD 1.6) vs 27.1 (SD 2.2), P=.01. No significant safety concerns reported.

## Limitations
- Nonrandomized, historical control cohort.
- Effect small (about 1 point); nonverbal skills not improved, so a supplement only.
- Details beyond abstract not verified.
