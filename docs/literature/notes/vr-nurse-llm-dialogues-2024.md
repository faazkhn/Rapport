## Mechanism
Script-generation system built with nurses, artists and developers for a Meta Quest 2 VR nurse trainer. LLMs (ChatGPT, Gemini) generate dialogue scripts, scored by lexical matching, semantic similarity and sentiment analysis against references.

## Evidence
Reported "high reproducibility and strong correlation with human evaluation"; no numbers retrieved.

## Limitations
- Abstract fragments only.

- No numbers verified.
