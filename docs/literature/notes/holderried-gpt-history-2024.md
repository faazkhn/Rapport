# Holderried: GPT-Powered Chatbot as a Simulated Patient to Practice History Taking

- **Citation:** Holderried F, et al., JMIR Medical Education 10:e53961, 2024. DOI 10.2196/53961

## Mechanism
GPT-3.5 chatbot prompted with a chatbot-optimised illness script plus a behavioural component. Medical students practised history taking; question-answer pairs were classified by whether the script covered them.

## Evidence
28 students, 826 QA pairs (median 27.5 per conversation); 94.7% about history taking. Script-covered questions (60.3%): 94.4% answered from explicit script. 23.4% of questions off-script. Implausible answers rare, biased to socially desirable.

## Limitations
- GPT-3.5, single scenario.
