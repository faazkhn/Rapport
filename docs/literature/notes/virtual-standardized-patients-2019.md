# Maicher 2019: Using virtual standardized patients to accurately assess information gathering skills in medical students

- **Citation:** Maicher KR, Zimmerman L, Wilcox B, et al. Medical Teacher, 2019. DOI 10.1080/0142159X.2019.1616683
- **Link:** https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/0142159X.2019.1616683 · **Open access:** no (abstract only, via Europe PMC)
- **Code:** none found · **Code licence:** none
- **Paper licence:** publisher
- **Informs:** evaluation, feedback/pedagogy, expression/embodiment

## Mechanism
Virtual standardized patient (VSP): emotionally responsive 3D characters that converse by natural language. The system classifies each student question into history domains and summarises the encounter, giving immediate feedback; its automated grading was compared with trained human raters.

## Evidence
20 dialogs randomly chosen from 102 encounters, rated by 3 humans and the computer. Computer accuracy 87% vs 90% for humans; overall scores not different; inter-rater reliability high in 19 of 21 categories.

## Limitations
- Only 20 dialogs analysed.
- Pre-LLM question classification; checklist-style grading.
- Full text not read.

## For Rapport
**Verdict:** idea-only. Question-domain classification gives a cheap, auditable coverage score for the faculty/feedback layer without touching patient facts; no code.
