# Medical LLM evaluation scoping review: Analyzing evaluation methods for large language models in the medical field: a scoping review

- **Citation:** Lee J, Park S, Shin J, Cho B. BMC Med Inform Decis Mak 2024. DOI 10.1186/s12911-024-02709-7
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1186/s12911-024-02709-7 · **Open access:** yes (PMC full text, first 100k characters read)
- **Code:** none found · **Code licence:** none
- **Paper licence:** CC BY 4.0
- **Informs:** evaluation

## Mechanism
Scoping review of PubMed, Embase and MEDLINE, articles from 1 Jan to 30 Sep 2023, coding how LLMs in medicine were evaluated: method type, number of questions, evaluators, repeats, extra analyses, prompt engineering.

## Evidence
142 articles. Test-exam evaluations 53 (37.3%); evaluation by medical professionals 80 (56.3%); hybrid 5 (3.5%); combination 4 (2.8%). Professional-evaluation studies mostly used 50 or fewer queries (54, 64.3%) and two evaluators (43, 48.3%). Exam studies: 15 (24.2%) repeated measurements, 8 (12.9%) used prompt engineering.

## Limitations
- Excludes open-source models and covers only three databases and nine months of 2023.
- No qualitative quality appraisal.
- Not about patient simulation.

## For Rapport
**Verdict:** idea-only. Confirms that small panels of two raters and few repeats are the norm; our evaluation plan should state rater count and repeated runs explicitly.
