# LLM patient simulation with structured feedback RCT: LLMs improve clinical decision making of medical students through patient simulation and structured feedback

- **Citation:** Brügge E, Ricchizzi S, Arenbeck M, et al. (Darici corresponding), BMC Medical Education 24:1391, 2024. doi:10.1186/s12909-024-06399-7 (the brief's "BMJ Health & Care Informatics" label is wrong).
- **Link:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11605890/ · **Open access:** yes (PMC page read)
- **Code:** none found · **Code licence:** none
- **Paper licence:** CC BY
- **Informs:** feedback/pedagogy, evaluation

## Mechanism
Students took histories from ChatGPT 3.5 patients (German, four neuro/neurosurgical scenarios). The intervention group also got AI-generated structured feedback after each case; control got none. Transcripts were scored with the CRI-HTI inventory by two blinded raters.

## Evidence
Randomised 24 of 29, analysed 21 (feedback n=10, control n=11). CRI-HTI after four cases: 3.604 vs 3.017 on 5 points (p=.049, partial eta2 0.198); baseline 3.28 vs 3.21. Significant for creating context (p=.046) and securing information (p=.018), not focusing questions (p=.265). Rater ICC 0.924. Students rated accuracy 8.67/10, realism 7.81/10.

## Limitations
- Very small sample; borderline p-value; abstract and results report different F degrees of freedom.
- ChatGPT 3.5 non-reproducible; no non-verbal cues.
- Somatic scenarios, German only.

## For Rapport
**Verdict:** adapt. Supports an end-of-case structured debrief scored against a history-taking rubric; the feedback component would sit beside the Twin (post-encounter, outside the live call), with scoring fixed by code not learning.
