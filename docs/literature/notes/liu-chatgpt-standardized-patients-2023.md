# Liu et al.: ChatGPT: when the artificial intelligence meets standardized patients in clinical training

- **Citation:** Liu X, Wu C, Lai R, et al., J Transl Med 2023 (letter). DOI 10.1186/s12967-023-04314-0 (PMC10326922).
- **Link:** https://translational-medicine.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12967-023-04314-0 · **Open access:** yes (PMC)
- **Code:** none · **Code licence:** none
- **Paper licence:** CC BY
- **Informs:** evaluation

## Mechanism
Letter to the editor: gpt-3.5-turbo role-played standardized patients for 10 patient cases (11-15 questions each) while a physician took histories; five senior physicians scored accuracy.

## Evidence
0-10 accuracy by 5 physicians over 10 cases: two scored 10, six scored 9, two scored 8. Cases 9 and 12 gave mechanical, rigid answers to multi-part questions.

## Limitations
- Tiny sample, subjective unexplained scoring.
- No comparison with real SPs or learner outcomes; savings are asserted.
- Rigid with compound questions.

## For Rapport
**Verdict:** skip. Weak evidence; only note that compound doctor questions need explicit handling (exam-command router / one-question-at-a-time policy).
