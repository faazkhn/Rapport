# Gutierrez Maquilon 2024: Integrating GPT-Based AI into Virtual Patients for Medical First Responders

- **Citation:** Gutierrez Maquilon R, Uhl J, Schrom-Feiertag H, Tscheligi M. JMIR Form Res 8:e58623, 2024. DOI 10.2196/58623
- **Link:** https://formative.jmir.org/2024/1/e58623/PDF · **Open access:** abstract only (fetch empty)
- **Code:** none found · **Code licence:** unknown
- **Paper licence:** CC BY (JMIR, believed)
- **Informs:** expression/embodiment, evaluation

## Mechanism
Mixed-reality traffic-accident simulation with a ChatGPT-driven virtual patient, prompted with verified accident-victim characteristics. Speech interface; usability questionnaires.

## Evidence
24 first responders; questionnaires on voice quality and speech-interface usability (scores not retrieved). Conclusion: AI helps but verbal training still needs instructional support.

## Limitations
- Abstract only.
- Small n, no control.
- Emergency, not psychiatric, setting.

## For Rapport
**Verdict:** idea-only. Supports pairing the voice agent with instructor scaffolding (feedback layer); voice-quality questionnaire could be reused for pilot testing.
