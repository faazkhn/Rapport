## Mechanism
Narrative review of embodied virtual patients for communication training in psychiatry (depression, suicide risk, PTSD, breaking bad news) and geriatrics, covering design, realism and trainee assessment, and introducing VirtuAlz for Alzheimer's care. Ends with recommendations for building, running and assessing VP sessions.

## Evidence
14 tools across 17 articles. Five tools used non-conversational formats (prerecorded video plus text); four had natural-language interaction on large screens; natural-language tools mostly relied on a Wizard-of-Oz operator. Studies mostly small (about 15 to 35 participants) and measured feasibility or satisfaction. RT-SIM knowledge gains declined at follow-up.

## Limitations
- Mostly feasibility studies, few learning outcomes or transfer data.
- Learner non-verbal behaviour rarely assessed.
- Pre-LLM (2022) technology.

## For Rapport
**Verdict:** idea-only. Justifies embodiment (non-verbal cues shape alliance) and flags the gap Rapport fills: autonomous speech without a wizard; consider assessing doctor non-verbal behaviour later in feedback.
