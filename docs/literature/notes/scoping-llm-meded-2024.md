# Aster scoping review: ChatGPT and Other Large Language Models in Medical Education, Scoping Literature Review

- **Citation:** Aster A, et al., Medical Science Educator, 2024. DOI 10.1007/s40670-024-02206-6
- **Link:** https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11933646/ · **Open access:** yes (abstract and search summary only read)
- **Code:** none found · **Code licence:** none
- **Paper licence:** unknown (open access, likely CC BY)
- **Informs:** none

## Mechanism
PRISMA scoping review of LLM publications in medical education over the first year of ChatGPT. Five databases searched; studies charted by use case.

## Evidence
1509 initial results, 145 studies included. Most studies tested LLM performance on exams; few were empirical education studies.

## Limitations
- Covers only the first year (2023-24).
- Many included studies were methodologically weak.
- Not specific to simulated patients.

## For Rapport
**Verdict:** skip. Background context only; no mechanism for the Digital Twin. Useful to cite that rigorous evaluation of LLM tutors is scarce.
