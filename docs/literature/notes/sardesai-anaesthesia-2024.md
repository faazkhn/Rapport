# Sardesai: Generative conversational AI for simulated patient encounters, anaesthesia training pilot

- **Citation:** Sardesai N, Russo P, Martin J, Sardesai A, Postgraduate Medical Journal 100(1182):237-241, 2024.
- **Link:** https://academic.oup.com/pmj/article/100/1182/237/7571383 · **Open access:** abstract only (summary page)
- **Code:** none · **Code licence:** none
- **Paper licence:** unknown
- **Informs:** expression/embodiment, evaluation

## Mechanism
No-code Convai platform; virtual patient "Sarah Adams" with backstory and custom knowledge bank. Speech in, spoken avatar reply, 2D browser; 3D Unreal integration described but untested.

## Evidence
n=15 (11 consultants, 4 juniors), 1-10 Likert. Intuitiveness median 9 (IQR 7-10); response accuracy median 8 (7-10); comfort 13/15; feasibility to replace actors median 9.

## Limitations
- Tiny, consultant-heavy sample, no objective outcomes.
- Replies too long, formal, polite, repetitive.
- Answers varied between sessions; hallucination.

## For Rapport
**Verdict:** idea-only. Their complaints (verbose, over-polite) are exactly what the single structured call with utterance length limits should address; useful negative baseline.
