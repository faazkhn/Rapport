# PsychSim animations (Phase A)

Generated by `tools/blender/make_clips.py` (usage line at the top of the script). Re-running it rebuilds every FBX, the face JSONs and the preview stills from scratch. Its `verify` mode re-imports each FBX and checks it.

All clips use the CC3 skeleton (101 `CC_Base_*` bones, the same on Mégane `female_20s_a` and Matt `male_20s_a`). They were authored on Mégane. Each FBX holds the armature only (no meshes), with every frame baked as a key, at 30 fps. The unit is the same as the character FBX: centimetre bones under an `Armature` node at scale 0.01, so 1 Unity unit = 1 m. The file's default pose is the CC3 A-pose rest pose, so Unity can build an avatar from the file. The FBX take name is the clip name.

Every gesture and cue clip carries a full-body pose (the `base_sit_neutral` frame 0) underneath the moving part, so it plays correctly on its own as well. Every loop's last frame equals its first frame. Gaze, tremor and slowed motion are not animated; code drives them.

Face presets are in `Face/`: `face_neutral`, `face_sad`, `face_flat`, `face_anxious`, `face_ashamed`, plus `face_squint` for `action_look_at_light`. Each file has the form `{"mesh": "CC_Base_Body", "weights": {...}}` on Unity's 0–100 scale. Every preset lists every key that any preset uses (most at 0), so playing a preset clip fully replaces the previous one. All the key names were checked against `CC_Base_Body`. The `Eye_Blink_*` lid drop has same-named keys on `CC_Base_EyeOcclusion` and `CC_Base_TearLine`. Drive those with the same weights in Phase B, otherwise the occlusion shadow will not follow the lid.

## Unity import (Rig = Humanoid)

- **Avatar:** use *Copy From Other Avatar* with the character's avatar (the hierarchy and names are identical: `Armature/CC_Base_BoneRoot/CC_Base_Hip/...`). *Create From This Model* also works, because the file's default pose is the A-pose.
- **Root:** `CC_Base_BoneRoot` never moves. The humanoid Hips bone is `CC_Base_Hip`, and all body translation is on it. Humanoid root motion comes from the body centre (Hips), so leave *Root node* empty and use the Root Transform settings below.
- **Forward:** in Blender she faces −Y. The files are exported with Forward −Z and Up Y (the same as the character FBX), so in Unity she faces **+Z** and `walk_in` walks along +Z.
- **Scale:** File Scale 1 (the FBX already declares centimetres, and Unity converts to 1 unit = 1 m).
- Set the clip range to the whole take (0..N).

*Based Upon: Original* matters for every seated clip. Without it, Unity re-centres the body over the GameObject and the pelvis leaves the chair.

### Placing the character at the chair
- The seated clips put the hips 0.40 m **behind** the GameObject origin, with the seat top at 0.45 m. The reference chair seat runs from 0.10 m to 0.56 m behind the origin, and the feet rest 0.12–0.16 m in front of it. Put `PatientAnchor` (the character's GameObject position and rotation) on the floor 0.10 m in front of the seat's front edge, facing away from the backrest. `sit_down` starts standing on that same spot.
- `walk_in` ends standing with the feet together. Rotate and place the character so the walk ends on the `PatientAnchor`, then cross-fade (0.2–0.3 s) into `sit_down` → `base_sit_neutral`.
- Turn on **Foot IK** on the seated and `sit_down` states. Matt's legs are 3–4 % longer than Mégane's and he wears flat shoes, while she wears heels. Without Foot IK his feet hover a few cm, and his pelvis sits about 1–1.5 cm low (humanoid scaling compensates for part of this).

### Layering
- **Head clips** (`gesture_nod`, `gesture_head_shake`): override layer, Head mask. They work over both bases.
- **`cue_wipe_tears`**: override layer, Arms mask. Over `base_sit_neutral` the fingers pass about 6 mm in front of the lower lid. Over `base_sit_slumped`, where the head is lower, they touch the cheek and press up to about 3–4 mm into it for about 0.3 s. Don't play it additively: the large arm motion breaks the additive blend.
- **Talk gestures and the fidget**: as override layers (Arms mask / Hands mask) they are exact on `base_sit_neutral`. Over `base_sit_slumped` the override puts the hands into the thighs, because the arm pose was authored on an upright chest. To use them over the slumped base, set the layer to **Additive**. Every one of these clips starts on the neutral lap pose, which serves as the additive reference (first frame). This was checked in Blender: the additive result stays on the lap.
- Masks: Hands = Left/Right Fingers. Arms = Left/Right Arm (shoulder → hand) plus fingers. Head = Head + Neck.

## How it was checked
- Each FBX was re-imported into an empty scene (`make_clips.py -- verify ...`). Each one has a single armature with 101 `CC_Base_*` bones that match Mégane's names and rest positions (within 1 cm), the action `<clip>`, frames 0..N at 30 fps, and the hip travel listed above (`walk_in` −3.750 m along forward, seated loops 0).
- The re-imported actions were applied to the original Mégane mesh, and the lowest point of the body and trousers over the seat was measured. `base_sit_neutral` reads 0.445–0.447 m and `base_sit_slumped` 0.450–0.451 m, against a seat at 0.450 m. That is a 3–5 mm settle, or flush.
- During `walk_in`, the analytic IK reaches every ankle target (no foot sliding, no over-stretch).
- The fingertip-to-face distance was measured through the wipe (see above).
- Stills (`previews/`, 3 views each: front, 3/4, side, with the 0.45 m box chair) are rendered for every clip. There are close-ups of each face preset and a Matt spot check (`matt_*.png`: `base_sit_neutral`, `sit_down`, `base_sit_slumped`).

## Known weaknesses
- These are keyframed and procedural, not motion capture. The walk is believable at a room's distance, but the foot roll is simplified (no toe-off pivot on the toe joint itself in heels).
- The fidget reads as finger motion at 1.5 m. The thumb-to-nail contact is approximate and not collision-checked.
- In Blender the arm twist bones (`*Twist*`) are not driven, so the wrist skin can twist slightly at extreme hand rolls. Unity's humanoid twist settings take care of this.
- The seated hip height is an 8 mm compromise between the two bodies (Mégane settles slightly more, Matt slightly less). Every clip has `matt_*` previews; Matt was checked visually for all clips, including `base_sit_slumped` (no chair clipping, no T-poses, hands stay out of the thighs).
- The exam actions are override layers over `base_sit_neutral`; over `base_sit_slumped` the shoulders start lower, so blend in over 0.3–0.5 s.
