# Draft test script: a good handling of the 'I want to go home' station (faculty to review).
Hi, I'm one of the doctors on the ward. I hear you'd like to go home today. Can you tell me what's going on?
That sounds exhausting, three nights of hardly any sleep with all that noise. What else is making you want to leave?
I can see why you're worried about work. Can I ask about the night you came in, on the terrace?
Thank you for telling me. Are you still having thoughts of ending your life, especially at night?
Who would be at home with you if you went back tonight?
You came in as an independent patient, so you do have the right to ask to leave. Because of what happened on the terrace and these thoughts, the law lets us ask you to stay up to a day while we assess things properly. I'd much rather we decide this together.
In your own words, what do you think could happen if you went home alone tonight?
What would make one more night here bearable? A quieter bed, a call to your mother?
Let's do that. I'll ask for the side room and you can call your mother now. Shall we review it together with her tomorrow morning?
