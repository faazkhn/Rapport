# Target journals (checked 2026-11-14)

**Quartiles** are Scimago SJR 2025, the latest release. They come from the full Scimago export (downloaded from scimagojr.com on 2026-09-26),
because scimagojr.com blocks automated requests. "Q1" means Q1 in at least one category; the Q1 categories are listed.
**Fees** were checked on the journal's own pages where they could be reached, and otherwise on DOAJ, as marked. Re-check both before
submitting, and note that Web of Science (JCR) quartiles can differ.
